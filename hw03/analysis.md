# HW3 Part 4: Corporate Events Timeline Analysis

**Question:** Based on the data, do executive changes precede or follow earnings announcements? Is there a pattern across the five companies?

Across the five companies, executive changes more often came *before* an earnings announcement than after: 21 of 29 events (11 of 16 separate 8-K filings) were filed before the nearest earnings release, 6 after, and 2 in the same week. The pattern varies by company. Walmart and JPMorgan filed all of their changes in the weeks leading up to earnings, including Walmart's CEO succession (Doug McMillon to John Furner), which was filed just 6 days before its third-quarter results (so it counts as "same week"). Apple's biggest change (Tim Cook handing the CEO role to John Ternus) also came 10 days before earnings, while Microsoft's events, all board-level changes, mostly came after. Because earnings are released every quarter, any date falls within about 45 days of some earnings filing, so with only 16 filings from five companies this lean toward "before earnings" is suggestive rather than proof of a deliberate timing strategy.

| Timing | By person | By 8-K filing |
|---|---|---|
| Before earnings | 21 | 11 |
| After earnings | 6 | 4 |
| Same week (within 7 days) | 2 | 1 |
| **Total** | **29** | **16** |

*Note: three other Item 5.02 filings (Microsoft Dec 2025, NVIDIA Mar 2026, JPMorgan Jan 2026) were only about pay (a new stock plan, an executive bonus plan, and Jamie Dimon's 2025 compensation), with no one joining or leaving, so they are not counted as executive changes.*
